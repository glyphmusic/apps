# Glyph 0.12.9 - 2026-11-19

- **Android**: artists, playlists and the start no longer get stuck, and the app no longer freezes after you come back from another app.
- **Much faster artist pages and lists**: an artist opens in well under a second (at once the second time), Liked Songs and playlists in a blink. The Glyph server now keeps artist pages for everyone, and the app keeps them too, also offline.
- **Share menu on phones**: sharing opens a menu with WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, e-mail and more.
- **Share songs to Glyph** and **open music files with Glyph**: pick Liked Songs or playlists for a shared song; play MP3, FLAC and other files with "Open with".
- **Copied music links** (computer): copy a song or playlist link elsewhere and Glyph offers to add it.

See CHANGELOG.md for the complete changes.
