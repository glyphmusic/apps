# Glyph 0.7.0

- Plugins: Settings > Plugins installs themes and extensions from GitHub (listed in glyphmusic/plugins or tagged glyph-plugin), at your own risk. Extensions run sandboxed and only with the permissions you confirm. Guide: docs.glyph.my/plugins.
- Settings > System: start Glyph with your computer, optionally in the background with a tray icon.
- macOS gets a `.dmg` (Glyph.app), Linux a `.deb` and an `.rpm`; the portable archives stay. The Windows uninstaller asks what to do with your data.
- New installs play YouTube locally with r-dlp; phone volume keys control the network speaker you play on.
- Fixed: phone playback stopping between songs in the background, flinging scrolls, and the player getting stuck after the sound output changed.
- Android: `glyph-0.7.0-android.apk` installs over 0.6.1 (Settings > About).

See CHANGELOG.md for the complete list.
