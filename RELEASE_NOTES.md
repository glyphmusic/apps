# Glyph 0.12.8 - 2026-11-18

- **Android**: the app no longer freezes when you leave it and come back.
- **Animations setting** (Settings > Appearance): choose whether the graphics card (GPU) or the processor (CPU) draws the background. The graphics card is the default and now does all of it, so the processor stays almost free.
- **Faster**: songs start sooner, search starts sooner and the first hit plays at once, and the app does much less work while a song plays.
- **Phone layout**: the page shows again under the tab bar and the player.

See CHANGELOG.md for the complete changes.
