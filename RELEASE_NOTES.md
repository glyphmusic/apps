# Glyph 0.12.6 - 2026-11-18

- **Performance mode**: a new switch in Settings > Appearance makes the background calmer and turns off blur and text effects, for older computers, laptops on battery and phones. The background's characters are now drawn by the graphics card, which takes load off the processor.
- **Faster pages**: albums, Home and artist pictures are remembered, so they show at once. The web player keeps big libraries after a reload.
- **Imports keep their order**: imported liked songs no longer show up shuffled on your other devices.
- **Covers**: sidebar icons and covers no longer get stuck as placeholders.
- **Android**: the app recovers by itself from a black screen after coming back, and playback no longer stops when the network drops for a moment.
- **Settings**: streaming quality is shown as picture cards with how much data an hour uses.

See CHANGELOG.md for the complete changes.
