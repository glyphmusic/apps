# Glyph 0.6.1

- Your other devices show song, pause, volume and jumps within a moment (needs Glyph Server 0.6.1).
- A song that cannot play is skipped instead of stopping the music; undo after removing songs or playlists.
- Sleep timer fades out; radio goes on from the end of a list; search results stop jumping.
- Phones: list names stay in the top bar, sheets close by pulling, long-press submenus as sheets.
- Android: `glyph-0.6.1-android.apk` installs over 0.6.0 (Settings > About).

See CHANGELOG.md for the complete list.
